Celebrating India's Festivals

India is a land of many festivals. Here you can learn about some famous festivals celebrated across the country.

Popular Festivals

Diwali

Diwali is the festival of lights. People light diyas, decorate their homes and share sweets.

Watch Diwali videos

Holi

Holi is the festival of colours. People play with colours and water with friends and family.

Watch Holi videos

Eid

Eid is celebrated by Muslims after the holy month of Ramadan with prayers and feasts.

Watch Eid videos

Pongal

Pongal is a harvest festival of Tamil Nadu. Homes are decorated with floor art called kolam (rangoli).

Watch Pongal videos

Traditions

  • Rangoli: colourful patterns made at the entrance of homes
  • Classical dances: Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi
  • Festival food: sweets and special dishes for every occasion
  • Folk music and storytelling

Watch Indian classical dance videos

Cultural Heritage

  • Taj Mahal, Agra
  • Hampi, Karnataka
  • Ajanta and Ellora Caves, Maharashtra
  • Konark Sun Temple, Odisha

Watch heritage site videos

Regional Celebrations

North: Lohri, Baisakhi

South: Onam, Pongal

East: Durga Puja, Rath Yatra

West: Ganesh Chaturthi, Garba
